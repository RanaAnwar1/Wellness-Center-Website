Frequently Asked Questions

How do I schedule a fitness assessment?
You can schedule a fitness assessment by calling us at (555) 123-4567, or by visiting our front desk. The assessment takes approximately 45 minutes and helps us create a personalized fitness plan for you.
Staff Recommended
What should I bring to my first visit?
For your first visit, please bring a valid ID, comfortable workout clothes, appropriate shoes, a water bottle, and a towel. If you're coming for a specific class or session, check if there are any additional requirements. Lockers are available but please bring your own lock.
How do I cancel or reschedule an appointment?
To cancel or reschedule an appointment, please contact us at least 24 hours in advance. You can do this by calling us or through your online account. Late cancellations or no-shows may incur a fee equal to 50% of the service price.
Do you offer nutrition plans?
Yes, we offer personalized nutrition consultations with our certified nutritionists. They will create a custom meal plan based on your goals, preferences, and any dietary restrictions you may have. These consultations can be booked separately or as part of our Elite Wellness membership package.
Most Popular Service
What's your policy on guests?
Members can bring guests depending on their membership level. Basic members get 1 guest pass per month, Premium members get 3, and Elite members have unlimited guest privileges. All guests must sign a waiver and be accompanied by the member at all times. Guest passes expire at the end of each month and do not roll over.
